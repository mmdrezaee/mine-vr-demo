Hazard recognition training for open-pit mining

Open a demo in the Meta Quest Browser and tap Enter VR, or play it on a laptop with the mouse. Nothing to install.

V3

Live shift with Sam, a trainer you can talk to

Everything in V2, plus positional machinery sound (haul truck engines, excavator hydraulics, bucket dumps, reversing alarms, wind) and Sam, a virtual shift trainer who narrates, reacts to what you do and answers questions about the procedure.

 V2

From documents to immersive training

A live shift at the pit lookout: moving haul trucks, an excavator loading, light vehicles. The rules of a written procedure become live situations, with points, streaks, badges and a learner profile that adapts the next shift.

 V1

Open-pit bench inspection

A static bench beside a haul road with five hazards to find. Records time to find each hazard, false alarms and when each hazard was first looked at.
