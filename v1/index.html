Hazard recognition training · prototype

Open-pit bench inspection

You are on a mine bench beside a haul road, just before the shift starts. Find the 5 hazards around you. Some things look risky but are not, and wrong picks count as false alarms.

The app records time to find each hazard, false alarms, and when you first looked at each one.

Quest 3: point and pull the trigger to mark a hazard. Left stick walks, right stick turns.
Screen: drag to look, W A S D to walk (Shift to run), click to mark a hazard, F to finish.
