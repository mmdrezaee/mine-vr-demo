Version 3 · sound and a trainer you can talk to

A live shift with Sam, your trainer

Safety rules usually live in long procedures. Here the same rules become a live shift you play, with the sound of the pit around you and Sam, a virtual shift trainer who explains, reacts to what you do and answers your questions.

  1. Personalize: pick who is training.
  2. The usual way: look at the procedure you would normally read.
  3. Turn it into training: spot hazards as they happen, earn points, streaks and badges. The next shift adapts to what you missed.
  4. Ask Sam: point at a question beside Sam, or type and talk on a computer.
