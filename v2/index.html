Version 2 · from documents to immersive training

A live shift at the pit lookout

Safety rules usually live in long procedures. Here the same rules become a live shift you play: haul trucks on the move, an excavator loading, light vehicles passing.

  1. Personalize: pick who is training.
  2. The usual way: look at the procedure you would normally read.
  3. Turn it into training: spot hazards as they happen, earn points, streaks and badges. The next shift adapts to what you missed.
Quest 3: point and pull the trigger. Left stick moves you on the lookout, right stick turns.
Screen: drag to look, click to choose or to mark a hazard.
